US state borders

gz_2010_us_040_00_5m.zip

In this one case, geopandas chokes on the file unless it's unzipped first.gz_2010_us_040_00_5m

There are 500k, 5m and 40m versions, with the 500k being the most detailed and the 40m the least.  The 040 part refers to the 50 States + territories (including Asian Pacific).  One can also get the counties.
