The notebook (in Safari) has its own menu bar.
You can get it into a state where this menu bar is obscured.

1.png

Quitting and relaunching will not fix it.

The issue is the zoom level.
Its counterintuitive, but the zoom level is too small.
It must be increased back to 100% or greater.

Sometimes CMD+ fails to change the zoom.  Sometimes it works.
