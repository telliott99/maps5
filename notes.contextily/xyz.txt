At zoom level n, there should be 2^n tiles
in each row and column

So if zoom = 10
2^10 = 1024 
360 degrees of LON total
each tile is about 360/1024 0.35 degrees
23 miles

At zoom = 11
1 tile with about 
0.75 deg LONG and LAT

We should have 2**11 = 2048 tiles hoz and vert
Resolution should be 360/2048 for LONG ≈ 0.175

tile numbering

https://www.thunderforest.com/docs/tile-numbering/

The tiles are numbered as {z}/{x}/{y}, where z is zoom, x is the tile number from left to right, and y is the tile number from top to bottom.

top level is zoom 0 with a single tile for the whole world
0/0/0

so at zoom level 10 there should be 2^10 = 1024 in each direction
a little less than 3 per degree of LON/LAT

Available zoom levels are 0-22
